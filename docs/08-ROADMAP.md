- React app

- dashboard

- improve reliability
- refine pricing

## Phase 3 — Intelligence
Potential features:
- AI assistant
- smart stock suggestions
- sales insights
- natural-language business questions

## Phase 4 — Automation
Potential:
- WhatsApp
- SMS
- reminders
- n8n
- scheduled reports
- automated workflows

## Phase 5 — Scale
Potential:
- Node/Express services
- more complex business logic
- staff roles
- multi-branch
- advanced analytics
- integrations

## Rule
Do not build future phases early just because they sound impressive.

The current product should first become useful, reliable and easy to operate.
